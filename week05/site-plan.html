Apex Cut & Grooming

WDD 231 Individual Project Site Plan

1. Site Name

Site Name: Apex Cut & Grooming

Reasoning: "Apex" signifies the highest point of quality and craft, positioning the shop as a premier grooming destination. The name is concise, memorable, and clearly communicates the core business offerings to modern clients.

2. Site Purpose

The primary purpose of the Apex Cut & Grooming website is to serve as a digital front door for current and prospective clients. It provides an easy way to explore services, view clear pricing, learn about individual barbers and their specialties, and book appointments online.

Additionally, the site highlights shop culture, displays a gallery of recent haircut styles, and provides essential business information including location, operating hours, walk-in policies, and contact channels.

3. Target Audience & Scenarios

Target Audience: Local professionals, students, and families looking for high-quality, reliable hair and beard grooming in a welcoming, modern environment.

User Scenarios:

  • Scenario 1: "How much does a standard haircut and beard trim cost, and what services are included in the package?"
  • Scenario 2: "Can I check open time slots and book an appointment with my preferred barber for this Saturday afternoon?"
  • Scenario 3: "Where is the shop located, what are the parking options, and do you accept walk-ins?"

4. Typography

To ensure clear visual hierarchy and high readability across all screen sizes, two Google Fonts have been chosen:

  • Heading Font: Montserrat (Sans-serif) – Used for all site titles, main section headings (h1, h2, h3), and navigation elements. Its bold, structured letterforms convey precision and confidence.
  • Body Font: Roboto (Sans-serif) – Used for body paragraphs, service item descriptions, pricing lists, forms, and footer information due to its high legibility on mobile and desktop screens.

6. Core Web Features

  • Interactive Service Menu: Itemized list showing service duration, price, and included treatments.
  • Dynamic Booking Call-to-Action: Prominent links directing users to the scheduling interface.
  • Filterable Style Gallery: Visual portfolio showing cuts by hair type and length.
  • Responsive Navigation: Accessible slide-out mobile menu and persistent desktop header.
